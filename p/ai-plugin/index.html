AI Plugin

Brings AI-powered features directly into the WordPress admin and editing experience.

The AI Plugin is the user-facing layer. It takes the AI Client infrastructure and turns it into concrete features inside the WordPress admin and editor — inline AI assistance, content generation, smart editing, and other capabilities that make AI useful without leaving WordPress.

Think of it as the bridge between the framework (AI Client) and the actual experiences that editors, site admins, and content creators touch every day.
